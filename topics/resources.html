What "certified" means here Sections labelled Official NESA are published by NESA or the NSW Curriculum website and define the course. The Official source section links to the legislation and organisations behind concepts the syllabus names; each is the authority on its own topic, not on the syllabus. Links open in a new tab.
The Course Specifications are part of the course NESA’s Higher School Certificate Course Specifications set out the notation, methods and lists you are expected to know and use in the HSC exam. This site links to NESA’s official PDF and never hosts a copy, so you always read the current version.
