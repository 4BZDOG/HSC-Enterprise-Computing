C

Cloud Computing

Delivering computing services such as storage, servers, databases and software over the internet, so an enterprise rents capacity instead of owning and running all of its own hardware.

Example: A small business keeps its files in a cloud drive and pays each month for exactly the storage it uses.

See also: Enterprise

E

Enterprise

An organisation, such as a business, school, charity or government agency, that uses computing systems to achieve its goals.

Example: A local bakery that takes online orders and tracks its stock in a spreadsheet.

See also: Cloud Computing
↑ Back to Top
U

User Experience (UX)

The overall experience a person has when using a system: how easy it is to learn, how efficiently they can complete tasks and how they feel about it.

Example: A checkout that takes three taps instead of ten.
